What's covered

Backward-looking & forward-looking

Where you've been
  • Current net income — what you're actually taking home after expenses
  • Missed and outstanding brand deal payments — money you're owed that hasn't landed
  • Income stream breakdown — what's performing, what's inconsistent, what's being left on the table
  • 1099 exposure — who owes you one and what to do if it doesn't show up
Where you're going
  • Cash flow projection — what's expected based on current contracts and pipeline
  • Revenue projection through end of year — based on seasonality, active deals, and affiliate trends
  • Estimated tax liability — what to set aside quarterly so tax season doesn't blindside you
  • Budget framework — how to allocate irregular income so it actually goes somewhere
Explicitly not included

This engagement does not include tax preparation, tax filing, official bookkeeping, or investment advice. The Creator Clarity Report is financial clarity and decision support — not execution. Referrals to trusted CPAs and bookkeepers are available if needed.
